About

Technology, operations
and better processes.

My career combines a hands-on operational mindset with formal software engineering education and practical enterprise IT support experience. I progressed from customer facing sales coordination into inventory leadership, then into IT logistics and ultimately Service Desk Analyst responsibilities in a high compliance diplomatic technology environment.

The technical progression was deliberate. While working in logistics, I completed a Bachelor of Engineering in Software Engineering and deliberately built practical capability beyond the original role through collaboration with Systems Administration, Telecommunications, Radio, Infrastructure and Customer Engagement teams.

I am currently completing a Master of Business Informatics specialising in Business Analytics in Auckland, combining service desk and technology operations experience with analytics, automation, reporting and business process improvement.

Career Story
Operations foundation

It started with service, accuracy and operational discipline.

I began in customer facing sales coordination, developing strong habits in service, stakeholder communication, inventory awareness, reporting and operational problem-solving. I then progressed into inventory team leadership, where high volume operations, SAP ERP transactions, reconciliation, reporting, team supervision and continuous improvement became part of my daily work.

IT logistics foundation

Technology moved from the background to the centre of my work.

Joining the U.S. Embassy Sri Lanka in IT logistics placed technology assets, procurement, deployments, infrastructure projects and lifecycle controls at the centre of day-to-day operations. Managing devices and equipment across multiple mission locations gave me a practical understanding of how technology, people and operational continuity connect.

Technical development

I built the technical foundation while continuing to work.

While working as an IT logistics professional, I completed a Bachelor of Engineering in Software Engineering and deliberately sought cross-functional exposure. Assignments with Systems Administration, Telecommunications, Radio, Infrastructure and Customer Engagement expanded my hands-on experience into endpoint support, applications, networking, telephony, cabling, AV, mobile devices and technical troubleshooting.

Service Desk progression

The role evolved into frontline enterprise IT support.

I progressed into Service Desk Analyst responsibilities, combining structured troubleshooting with customer support, ServiceNow incident and service-request management, Microsoft identity and endpoint tools, user administration and technical project work. The progression was supported by formal education, practical capability and demonstrated performance in enterprise support, infrastructure and customer-facing troubleshooting.

Current direction

Now the focus is connecting technical delivery with data and business improvement.

Based in Auckland and completing a Master of Business Informatics in Business Analytics, I am continuing to build capability across Service Desk, IT Operations, Endpoint Support, Systems Support, Cloud, Networking, Cybersecurity and technology enabled business improvement. Power BI, Power Apps and Power Automate add an analytical and automation dimension to the practical support experience.
